EvaShort Support

Last updated: 2026-08-31

EvaShort is a short-drama streaming app operated by Haowei (shenzhen) Cultural Media Co., Ltd. Every episode is a few minutes long, so you can finish a whole story in one sitting. This page explains how to get help.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my VIP purchase?

Open the app, go to Profile → Restore Purchase. VIP is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, so you only ever pay once and can restore it on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. No extra account is needed to restore.

I was charged but the content is still locked.

First, force-close and reopen the app — an unfinished transaction is usually completed automatically on the next launch. If it is still locked, use Profile → Restore Purchase. If neither works, email us with the date of the charge and we will sort it out.

Can I try EvaShort without creating an account?

Yes. You can browse and watch in guest mode without registering. Some titles are marked VIP and require the one-time unlock. Creating a free account lets your saved list and watch history follow you when you reinstall the app.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account, username, saved list and watch history from our servers; the action is immediate and cannot be undone. Data stored only on your device can be cleared by logging out or uninstalling the app.

A video will not play, or keeps buffering.

EvaShort streams over the internet, so a weak connection is the usual cause. Try switching between Wi-Fi and cellular data, or moving closer to your router. If one specific episode fails while others play fine, email us the title and episode number.

Why do I only see a small number of titles?

EvaShort is new and we add licensed short dramas regularly. Every title in the app is properly licensed — we do not scrape or redistribute third-party content.
