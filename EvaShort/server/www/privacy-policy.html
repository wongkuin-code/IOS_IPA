Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31  ·  Effective: 2026-08-31

EvaShort (“we”, “us”) is a short-drama streaming app operated by Haowei (shenzhen) Cultural Media Co., Ltd. This policy explains what information we handle, why we handle it, and the choices you have. By using EvaShort you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

1.1 When you create an account

  • Username — 2–20 characters, chosen by you.
  • Password — stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We never store, and cannot see, your plain-text password.
  • Nickname and avatar — optional, chosen by you.
  • Account creation time.

We do not ask for or collect your email address, phone number, real name, or postal address.

1.2 When you use the app

  • Watch history and saved / favourite list.
  • Search history and app preferences such as light/dark theme.
  • Purchase status and transaction identifiers — used to verify and restore your VIP unlock.
Watch history, saved list, search history, theme preference and unlock status are stored on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers. If you sign in, your saved list and watch history are additionally synced to your account so that you can restore them after reinstalling the app.

1.3 Automatically recorded by our server

Like virtually every online service, our web server records standard request logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent). These are used solely for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. They are rotated and deleted periodically, and are never used for tracking, profiling or advertising.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Email address, phone number, real name, or postal address.
  • Precise or coarse location / GPS.
  • Contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • Health, fitness, financial or other sensitive information.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking identifier.

EvaShort contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • authenticate you and keep you signed in;
  • sync your saved list and watch history to your account;
  • verify and restore your in-app purchase;
  • deliver video content;
  • protect against abuse and diagnose technical problems.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Third parties

  • Apple — all payments are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never receive or store your payment card details. Apple provides us with a purchase transaction identifier, which we use only to confirm and restore your entitlement.
  • Hosting — the app communicates only with our own server (api.haoweimedia.cn), hosted in Hong Kong.

No other third party receives your data.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Account data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Server request logs are rotated and deleted periodically.
  • You can delete your data yourself at any time: open the app, go to Profile → Delete Account, and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account, username, password hash, saved list and watch history from our servers. The action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Information that lives only on your device (section 1.2) never reaches our servers, so it is not covered by account deletion. You can clear it by logging out or by uninstalling the app.

6. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, never in plain text, and sessions use bearer tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using commercially reasonable means.

7. Children's privacy

EvaShort is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the address below and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
