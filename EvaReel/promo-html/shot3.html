Before I Met My True Love
Ava Chen
EP.23 · 90 Episodes

After three years of marriage, she finally realized he never loved her.

The ring on her finger had grown cold, like the man who once swore to protect her. Every dinner was silent, every glance distant.

She told herself it was just work, just stress — until she saw the messages on his phone.

"Some love fades quietly, and one day you wake up and it's already gone."

Tonight she would finally ask him the question she had been too afraid to voice.
