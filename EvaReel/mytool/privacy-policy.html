Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

EvaReel respects and protects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Settings such as favorites and watch history are stored only in your device's local storage (AsyncStorage).

2. In-App Purchases

Purchases are completed through Apple's official in-app purchase system (App Store / StoreKit). This app cannot access your payment information; all payment processing is handled by Apple. Purchase records are stored only in your device's local storage and are used to restore purchased content.

3. Third-Party Services

The episode content inside the app is provided natively by the app and does not rely on any third-party content service. This app does not submit any of your personal information to any third party.

4. Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from children.
